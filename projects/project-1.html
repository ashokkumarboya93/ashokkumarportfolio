The Problem

Manual attendance tracking using paper registers is slow, insecure, and difficult to audit. It often leads to data entry errors, lost records, and lack of real-time visibility for both students and administration regarding attendance requirements.

The Solution

The Student Attendance Management System provides a digitized solution using PHP and MySQL. It features a secure portal for students to track their progress and an admin dashboard for faculty to mark attendance and generate automated compliance reports, ensuring transparency and data integrity.

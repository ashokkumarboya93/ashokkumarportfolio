The Problem

Manual monitoring of crowded public spaces is labor-intensive, prone to human error, and lacks scalability. Without automated density estimation, responding to potential safety risks like overcrowding or stampedes becomes reactive rather than proactive.

The Solution

Deep-Vision leverages Computer Vision and Deep Learning (YOLO/PyTorch) to automate crowd analysis in real-time. By providing live FPS metrics, density heatmaps, and automated alerts, the system reduces the need for manual monitoring by 60% while improving public safety through predictive analytics.

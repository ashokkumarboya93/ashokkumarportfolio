The Problem

Modern web applications are constantly under threat from Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks. Traditional threshold-based detection often fails to identify sophisticated, low-volume attacks or results in high false positives, leading to service downtime and lost revenue.

The Solution

This system implements an entropy-based detection engine that analyzes traffic randomness rather than just volume. By measuring the Shannon entropy of packet distributions, it can identify abnormal patterns with high precision, enabling real-time mitigation and automated IP blocking through a simulated environment.

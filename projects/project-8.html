Key Features

📈 Predictive Scoring

Heuristics-based system for identifying at-risk students and predicting potential dropouts early.

👤 Student 360° View

Unified profiles tracking attendance, academic history, risk scores, and progress metrics.

📊 Dynamic Analytics

Interactive Plotly.js visualizations for subject-wise and semester-wise performance drill-downs.

☁️ Cloud Integration

Scalable architecture with Supabase PostgreSQL backend and REST-based data communication.

The Problem

Institutions often fail to identify struggling students early because traditional systems only store raw data without generating insights. Faculty lack tools for real-time monitoring and predictive analytics, leading to high dropout rates and unaddressed academic gaps.

The Solution

EduMetric bridges this gap by transforming academic data into meaningful performance scores and risk levels. Using a Flask backend and Plotly visualizations, it empowers faculty with data-driven decision-making tools and early intervention strategies to ensure student success.
